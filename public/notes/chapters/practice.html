A. Journal, ledger idea, and trial balance

Practice A1 · Easy

Journalise four opening-week transactions

Ananya starts Ananya Mart on 1 April 2026:

  1. Introduced cash ₹80,000.
  2. Opened a bank account and deposited ₹50,000.
  3. Bought goods for cash ₹12,000.
  4. Sold goods for cash ₹7,000.

Pass journal entries with narrations.

Answer / solution

To Capital A/c80,000
(Being capital introduced in cash)
1 Apr 2026Bank A/c …… Dr.50,000
To Cash A/c50,000
(Being cash deposited into bank)

To Cash A/c12,000

B. Inventory — FIFO

Practice B1 · Easy

FIFO closing stock and COGS — one issue

Opening stock: 100 units @ ₹10. 5 January: purchased 200 units @ ₹12. 20 January: issued 150 units. Method: FIFO.

Find (i) COGS (ii) closing stock (qty and value). Prove that opening cost + purchases = COGS + closing stock.

Answer / solution

Step 1 — units. 100 + 200 − 150 = 150 units closing.

Step 2 — issue layers (oldest first). Issue 150 = 100 @ ₹10 + 50 @ ₹12.

100 × 10 = ₹1,000.   50 × 12 = ₹600.   COGS = ₹1,600.

Step 3 — remaining layers. From the 200 @ ₹12, 150 remain. Closing = 150 × 12 = ₹1,800.

Step 4 — rupee check. Opening 1,000 + purchases 2,400 = ₹3,400. COGS 1,600 + closing 1,800 = ₹3,400. Tick.

C. Inventory — Weighted Average (moving)

Practice C1 · Easy

Moving average — clean rate

Opening 100 units @ ₹20. Purchase 200 units @ ₹26. Then issue 150 units. Use perpetual weighted average.

Find average rate, issue value, closing stock.

Answer / solution

After purchase: units 100+200=300. Cost 100×20 + 200×26 = 2,000 + 5,200 = ₹7,200.

Average = 7,200 ÷ 300 = ₹24 exactly.

Issue 150 × 24 = ₹3,600 (COGS).

Closing 150 × 24 = ₹3,600.

Key point

The average was computed after the purchase, not after the issue.

Practice D3 · Moderate

SLM, Indian financial year, purchased 1 July

Plant purchased on 1 July 2023 for ₹2,40,000. Residual ₹24,000. Life 4 years. Books close on 31 March. SLM.

Compute depreciation for 2023–24, 2024–25 and 2025–26.

Answer / solution

Annual depreciation = (2,40,000 − 24,000) ÷ 4 = 2,16,000 ÷ 4 = ₹54,000.

2023–24: used from 1 July to 31 March = 9 months. 54,000 × 9/12 = 54,000 × 0.75 = ₹40,500.

2024–25: full year ₹54,000.

2025–26: full year ₹54,000.

Accumulated after these three periods = 40,500 + 54,000 + 54,000 = ₹1,48,500. WDV = 2,40,000 − 1,48,500 = ₹91,500.

Practice D4 · Exam-level

SLM with installation, part year, and sale

A machine was purchased on 1 October 2022 for ₹5,00,000. Installation ₹20,000. Residual value 10% of cost (including installation). Life 10 years. SLM. Year-end 31 March. The machine was sold on 30 September 2025 for ₹3,80,000.

Compute depreciation each year till sale and the profit or loss on sale.

Answer / solution

Cost = 5,00,000 + 20,000 = ₹5,20,000.

Scrap = 10% × 5,20,000 = ₹52,000.

Depreciable amount = 5,20,000 − 52,000 = ₹4,68,000.

Annual SLM = 4,68,000 ÷ 10 = ₹46,800.

2022–23 (1 Oct–31 Mar = 6 months): 46,800 × 6/12 = ₹23,400. WDV = 5,20,000 − 23,400 = ₹4,96,600.

2023–24: ₹46,800. WDV = 4,96,600 − 46,800 = ₹4,49,800.

2024–25: ₹46,800. WDV = 4,49,800 − 46,800 = ₹4,03,000.

2025–26 to 30 Sep = 6 months: ₹23,400. NBV at sale = 4,03,000 − 23,400 = ₹3,79,600.

Sale proceeds ₹3,80,000 − NBV ₹3,79,600 = Profit on sale ₹400.

Journal (sale): Bank Dr. 3,80,000; Accumulated depreciation Dr. 1,40,400; To Machine 5,20,000; To Profit on sale 400. (Accum. dep. = 23,400+46,800+46,800+23,400 = 1,40,400; 5,20,000 − 1,40,400 = 3,79,600 NBV.)

Practice G2 · Easy

Reconstruct CA and CL from current ratio and working capital

Current ratio = 2.5. Working capital = ₹1,50,000. Quick ratio = 1.5. There are no prepaid expenses. Find current assets, current liabilities and inventory.

Answer / solution

Let CL = x. Then CA = 2.5x.

Working capital = CA − CL = 2.5x − x = 1.5x = 1,50,000 ⇒ x = 1,50,000 / 1.5 = CL ₹1,00,000.

CA = 2.5 × 1,00,000 = ₹2,50,000.

Quick ratio 1.5 = (CA − Inventory) / CL ⇒ CA − Inventory = 1.5 × 1,00,000 = 1,50,000.

Inventory = 2,50,000 − 1,50,000 = ₹1,00,000.

Practice G3 · Difficult

From GP ratio and stock turnover, find sales

Gross profit ratio 20%. Inventory turnover 5 times (on COGS). Opening stock = closing stock = ₹40,000. Find COGS, gross profit and sales.

Answer / solution

Average stock = (40,000+40,000)/2 = ₹40,000.

Inventory turnover 5 = COGS / 40,000 ⇒ COGS = 5 × 40,000 = ₹2,00,000.

GP ratio 20% means GP = 20% of sales and COGS = 80% of sales.

GP = 2,50,000 − 2,00,000 = ₹50,000 (which is 20% of 2,50,000).
