Chapter A

Master Formula Sheet

Learning outcome. Every formula you need in the examination, in one place. Know what each symbol means before you memorise.

What this chapter covers (official syllabus)

  • Accounting equation
  • Inventory (FIFO, WAM, COGS)
  • Depreciation SLM & WDV
  • Company accounts workings
  • Cash flow derivations
  • All ratios

Copy this sheet once into your rough notes. In the hall, write the formula first, then substitute, then compute. Marks are often given for the formula even if arithmetic slips.

1. Accounting equation

Formula

Assets = Liabilities + Capital

Formula

Assets = Liabilities + Opening capital + Profit − Loss − Drawings + Additional capital

Profit = Revenue − Expenses. Drawings reduce capital, they are not a business expense.

Formula

Closing capital = Opening capital + Additional capital + Profit − Drawings

2. Inventory and trading

Formula

Cost of goods sold (COGS) = Opening stock + Purchases + Direct expenses − Closing stock

Formula

Gross profit = Sales − COGS   (or Sales + Closing stock − Opening stock − Purchases − Direct expenses)

Formula

Net profit = Gross profit + Other income − Indirect expenses

FIFO

Rule. Issues are taken from the oldest layer still in stock. Closing stock is the latest layers that remain.

Formula

Closing stock (FIFO) = remaining quantity in each leftover layer × that layer’s purchase cost

Formula

COGS (FIFO) = cost of layers issued  |  Check: Opening cost + Purchases cost = COGS + Closing stock

Weighted average (WAM)

Formula

Weighted average cost = Total cost of units on hand ÷ Total units on hand

Perpetual / moving average (usual exam method): recompute the average after every purchase. Issues go out at the average then ruling. Do not recompute on an issue.

Periodic average: one average for the whole period using opening + all purchases, then closing quantity × that average.

Formula

Unit check: Opening qty + Purchases qty − Issues qty = Closing qty

3. Depreciation

Formula

Depreciable amount = Cost − Residual (scrap) value

Cost includes purchase price, non-refundable taxes, freight inward, and installation needed to bring the asset to use.

Straight Line Method (SLM) / Original cost / Fixed instalment

Formula

Annual depreciation = (Cost − Residual) ÷ Useful life in years

Formula

SLM rate on original cost = (Annual depreciation ÷ Cost) × 100

Formula

Part-year depreciation = Annual depreciation × (Months of use ÷ 12)

Written Down Value (WDV) / Diminishing / Reducing balance

Formula

Depreciation for the year = Book value at the start of the period × Rate

Formula

Part-year WDV = Cost (or WDV) × Rate × (Months of use ÷ 12)

Formula

Optional WDV rate if life and scrap are given: R = 1 − (Scrap ÷ Cost)1/n

MBA papers almost always give the rate. Do not invent a rate.

Sale of asset

Formula

Net book value (NBV) on sale date = Cost − Accumulated depreciation till date of sale

Formula

Profit on sale = Sale proceeds − NBV   |   Loss on sale = NBV − Sale proceeds

4. Company accounts workings

Formula

Revenue from operations + Other income = Total income

Formula

Profit before tax = Total income − Total expenses ± Exceptional items

Formula

Profit after tax = Profit before tax − Tax expense

Formula

Changes in inventories = Opening inventory − Closing inventory (a positive figure is an expense)

Formula

Manager’s commission on profit after commission = Profit before commission × Rate ÷ (100 + Rate)

If the question says ‘on profit before commission’, just multiply profit × rate.

Formula

Trade receivables (net) = Gross receivables − Provision for doubtful debts

Formula

PPE (net) = Gross cost − Accumulated depreciation

Formula

Shareholders’ funds = Share capital + Reserves and surplus − Fictitious assets if any

Formula

New provision for doubtful debts = Required % × Debtors (after writing off further bad debts)

Formula

If an old provision already exists: P&L charge (or credit) = New provision − Old provision + Fresh bad debts

5. Cash flow (indirect method) — derivations

Formula

Net increase in cash = Operating (A) + Investing (B) + Financing (C)

Formula

Closing cash & cash equivalents = Opening + Net increase (must equal the Balance Sheet)

Formula

Tax paid = Opening tax provision + Current tax charged − Closing tax provision (adjust for prepaid tax if given)

Formula

Dividend paid = Opening proposed/unpaid dividend + Dividend declared − Closing unpaid dividend

Formula

Purchase of PPE (if missing) : Opening cost + Purchases − Cost of asset sold = Closing cost

Formula

Depreciation for the year (if missing): Opening accum. dep. + Dep. charged − Accum. dep. on asset sold = Closing accum. dep.

Formula

Sale proceeds of PPE = NBV of asset sold ± Profit/Loss on sale

6. Ratio analysis

Balance Sheet ratios (both figures from the Balance Sheet)

Formula

Current ratio = Current assets ÷ Current liabilities

Formula

Quick (acid-test) ratio = (Current assets − Inventory − Prepaid expenses) ÷ Current liabilities

Formula

Absolute liquid / cash ratio = (Cash + Cash equivalents + Marketable securities) ÷ Current liabilities

Formula

Debt–equity ratio = Long-term debt ÷ Shareholders’ funds

Formula

Proprietary ratio = Shareholders’ funds ÷ Total assets

Formula

Capital gearing ratio = (Preference capital + Long-term debt) ÷ Equity shareholders’ funds

Formula

Fixed assets to long-term funds = Net fixed assets ÷ (Shareholders’ funds + Long-term debt)

Formula

Current assets to proprietary funds = Current assets ÷ Shareholders’ funds

Revenue ratios (both figures from the P&L)

Formula

Gross profit ratio = (Gross profit ÷ Revenue from operations) × 100

Formula

Net profit ratio = (Profit after tax ÷ Revenue from operations) × 100

Formula

Operating profit ratio = (Operating profit (EBIT) ÷ Revenue from operations) × 100

Formula

Operating ratio = (COGS + Operating expenses) ÷ Revenue from operations × 100

Formula

Expense ratio = (Particular expense ÷ Revenue from operations) × 100

Combined ratios (P&L figure with a Balance Sheet figure)

Formula

Inventory turnover = COGS ÷ Average inventory

Formula

Average inventory = (Opening inventory + Closing inventory) ÷ 2

Formula

Inventory holding period (days) = 365 ÷ Inventory turnover

Formula

Debtors turnover = Credit sales ÷ Average trade receivables

Formula

Average collection period = 365 ÷ Debtors turnover

Formula

Creditors turnover = Credit purchases ÷ Average trade payables

Formula

Average payment period = 365 ÷ Creditors turnover

Formula

Working capital turnover = Revenue from operations ÷ Working capital

Formula

Working capital = Current assets − Current liabilities

Formula

Fixed asset turnover = Revenue from operations ÷ Net fixed assets

Formula

Capital turnover = Revenue from operations ÷ Capital employed

Formula

Capital employed = Shareholders’ funds + Long-term debt (or Net fixed assets + Working capital)

Formula

ROCE = (EBIT ÷ Capital employed) × 100

Formula

ROE = (Profit after tax ÷ Equity shareholders’ funds) × 100

Formula

Return on assets = (PAT ÷ Total assets) × 100

Formula

EPS = Profit after tax (less preference dividend) ÷ Number of equity shares

Formula

Interest coverage = EBIT ÷ Interest

Exam tip

If opening figures are not given, use closing balances for turnover ratios and write the assumption. Never mix a % ratio with a ‘times’ ratio without stating the unit.
