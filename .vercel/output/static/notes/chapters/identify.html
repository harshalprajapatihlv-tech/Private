Chapter C

Numerical Question Identification Guide

Learning outcome. The fastest marks in accounting come from recognising the question type in the first 20 seconds.

What this chapter covers (official syllabus)

  • Journal / ledger / TB
  • Inventory
  • Depreciation
  • Company accounts
  • Cash flow
  • Ratios

Read the last sentence of the question first — that is usually the requirement. Then scan for trigger words in the table below.

Exam tip

If two methods are possible (FIFO vs WAM, SLM vs WDV) and the question is silent, write one line: ‘Assuming FIFO / SLM as commonly used’ — then proceed. Do not freeze.
